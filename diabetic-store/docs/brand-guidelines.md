## 0. Brand Core

**Name:** دودليزا — Dodoliza
**Category:** Premium comfort socks designed for people with diabetes.
**Promise (AR):** راحة تلبسها من الصبح لآخر اليوم.
**Promise (EN):** Comfort you can wear from morning to night.
**Positioning:** A wellness brand that treats the foot with care. It is not a pharmacy and it is not a medical-supply catalogue.
**Audience:**
- Primary: adults living with diabetes, mostly 45+, in Saudi Arabia.
- Secondary: sons, daughters and spouses who buy on their behalf.

**Reason to believe:**
1. Wide, non-binding top that does not leave marks on the leg.
2. Seamless toe with no ridge against the toes.
3. Bamboo fibers with silver woven into the yarn: breathable, absorbs moisture, keeps odor down.
4. Cushioned sole for long days on the feet.

## 4. Voice & Tone

**Personality:** calm, caring, expert, plain-spoken.

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| Reassuring | Alarmist |
| Specific | Vague or hypey |
| Respectful of older readers | Patronising |
| Honest about what socks can do | Making medical claims |

**Do:**
- Talk about comfort, protection, and everyday life: walking, prayer, work, travel.
- Explain the "why" in one short sentence per feature.
- Address the buyer warmly; it may be the wearer or their family.

**Don't:**
- Use fear ("before it's too late", wounds, amputation).
- Claim the product cures, treats, heals, or prevents any condition.
- Use slang-heavy or sales-shouting copy, emoji, or exclamation runs.

**Required note on product pages:**
AR: «إذا كانت لديك حالة خاصة في القدم، استشر طبيبك قبل الاستخدام.»
EN: "If you have a specific foot condition, consult your doctor."

## 5. Messaging Framework

**Tagline:** راحة تلبسها من الصبح لآخر اليوم. / All-day comfort, made with care.

**Pillars:**
1. **Gentle on the leg** — wide top that stays up without squeezing.
2. **Kind to the toes** — seamless toe, no rubbing.
3. **Fresh all day** — bamboo + silver fibers manage moisture and odor.
4. **Made for long days** — cushioned sole for walking and standing.

**Proof points (placeholders until confirmed):** fabric composition, care test results, customer reviews.

---

## 6. Imagery

- Close-ups: fabric weave, the wide top on the calf, the seamless toe, the cushioned sole.
- Lifestyle: adults 50+ walking, at home, at the mosque entrance, travelling. Natural light, real skin, unposed.
- Backgrounds: Surface white or Sage Mist. No clinical blue-white hospital look.
- Icons: Lucide line icons, 1.75px stroke, Teal Ink. No emoji.

---

## 7. Commerce Rules

- Currency: SAR («ر.س»). Show per-pair price on every pack option.
- Pack sizes: 3 / 6 / 12 pairs. Sizes: S (36–39), M (40–43), L (44–47).
- Payment: cash on delivery, cards, Apple Pay. Guest checkout always available.
- All prices, names and photos in the current build are placeholders.
